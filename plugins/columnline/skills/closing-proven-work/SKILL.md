# Review an outcome case study

## In plain English

Prepare the later case study: original purpose, outcomes, process changes, patterns, limits and lessons. Follow the stage boundary below and leave the next person a clear, truthful handoff.

## Entry

Live/accepted work, original intent and actual adoption/usage/results evidence with an agreed review context.

## Work and permitted actions

Prepare the later case study: original purpose, outcomes, process changes, patterns, limits and lessons. Distinguish result evidence from testimonials and delivery acceptance. Preserve explicit no-share instructions. A status, high usage, elapsed week or outcome-card display alone does not establish acceptance of this document. Do not activate a Proven/retirement worker or impose a universal observation window.

## Exit and exceptions

Actual scoped acceptance of the outcome/case-study document may support a deliberate Proven decision. Standing services continue; retirement requires a separate explicit decision.

Record actual friction and source evidence for a narrow reviewed correction. Never silently change stage semantics, approval policy, channel authority or enablement.
