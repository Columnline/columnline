# Capture and deduplicate client intake

## In plain English

Open the source and relevant attachments. Follow the stage boundary below and leave the next person a clear, truthful handoff.

Read [the overarching workflow](../driving-work-end-to-end/SKILL.md) and [shared authority/evidence rules](../driving-work-end-to-end/reference/system.md) first.

## Entry

A new source item in the correct client scope, plus the existing request/work ledger and recent receipts.

## Work and permitted actions

Open the source and relevant attachments. Classify new ask, existing-work update, simple Question, information-only or excluded. Preserve exact words, tenant and native source identity. Search before filing; reuse the existing canonical item. Route simple questions to handling-snapshot-questions. Do not send a text acknowledgment/answer, promise dates, prepare a plan, promote a project or start stage work. The optional explicitly authorized Question eyes reaction follows the Questions contract; it grants no other action.

## Exit and exceptions

A captured/deduplicated item and reason, source coverage and the appropriate shared-worker handoff. Internal intake remains stopped.

Record actual friction and source evidence for a narrow reviewed correction. Never silently change stage semantics, approval policy, channel authority or enablement.
