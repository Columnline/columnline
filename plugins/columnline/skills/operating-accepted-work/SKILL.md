# Keep accepted work live

## In plain English

Keep a working parent Live while linked child asks/fixes follow their own stages and scope acceptance. Follow the stage boundary below and leave the next person a clear, truthful handoff.

Read [the overarching workflow](../driving-work-end-to-end/SKILL.md) and [shared authority/evidence rules](../driving-work-end-to-end/reference/system.md) first.

## Entry

Actual delivery acceptance and the current functioning service or completed asset.

## Work and permitted actions

Keep a working parent Live while linked child asks/fixes follow their own stages and scope acceptance. Record relevant usage, adoption and feedback with sources. Completion of one issue never stops a standing worker/service. Recommend deliberate improvements or retirement for owner review; do not demote, shut down or infer Proven automatically.

## Exit and exceptions

Operating/completion context and any linked child improvement. Outcome/case-study review remains a separate evidence-led handoff.

Record actual friction and source evidence for a narrow reviewed correction. Never silently change stage semantics, approval policy, channel authority or enablement.
