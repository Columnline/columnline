# Prepare a delivery-acceptance package

## In plain English

Prepare a client-safe PDF describing the delivered result, use/verification, limits and requested acceptance. Follow the stage boundary below and leave the next person a clear, truthful handoff.

Read [the overarching workflow](../driving-work-end-to-end/SKILL.md) and [shared authority/evidence rules](../driving-work-end-to-end/reference/system.md) first.

## Entry

Actual delivery evidence, accepted scope/limits, canonical work and current Materials comments/revision history.

## Work and permitted actions

Prepare a client-safe PDF describing the delivered result, use/verification, limits and requested acceptance. Reuse the appropriate Material and preserve versions/comments. Link work and revision both ways where supported. Do not send, mark accepted or call this the later Proven case study. Research reports may already be the actual delivered document.

## Exit and exceptions

Prepared package and exact human review/send handoff. Its creation alone records no receipt or acceptance.

Record actual friction and source evidence for a narrow reviewed correction. Never silently change stage semantics, approval policy, channel authority or enablement.
