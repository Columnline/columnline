# Analyzing Columnline Sites

## In plain English

Read Sites traffic, inquiries, conversion and revenue evidence through Columnline. This follows the work your account can access and shows what the evidence actually proves.

## Workflow

Discover authorized Sites and analytics tools with `using-columnline`, then select the tenant and Site or saved list. Use the returned live analytics model rather than rebuilding a competing report from partial tables.

Choose comparable reporting windows and retain the returned timezone and metric definitions. Read source completeness and freshness before interpreting changes. Unavailable, delayed and partial data are not zero. Do not sum overlapping site totals, average rates without their denominators, or compare a partial period with a full period without making that limit clear.

Keep inquiries, booked meetings, held meetings, confirmed sales and collected cash separate. Use the source's adopted attendance and conversion rules; do not invent a new cohort model to answer a simple question. A financing commitment is not collected cash.

Lead with the actual business result, then the source-backed explanation and the next useful action. Include reporting window, denominator and missing source when they affect the conclusion. Use authorized lead history for drill-down, retaining Sites scope. If a requested source is missing, report the specific limitation and available evidence rather than inventing a total.
